## Use in Khaam
1. Copy into a video project: `python3 scripts/use_remotion_template.py audiogram <dest>`.
2. `npm install` inside the copied project (with consent).
3. Adapt fonts/colors/motion to Khaam taste and Arabic rules (`reference/arabic-typography.md`).
4. Preview still/short clip before final render; record versions/licences in delivery manifest.
