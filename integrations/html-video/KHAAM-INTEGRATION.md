## What it is
html-video: programmatic HTML/CSS/data to MP4 workflow with templates, pluggable render engines, and agent-friendly tooling.

## Key capabilities
- HTML/CSS to video rendering workflow
- Template catalog for common video formats
- Pluggable renderer/agent integration patterns
- Docs/research on programmatic video pipelines

## How to use in Khaam
Use templates/docs as reference for HTML-based video generation patterns. Good for quick programmatic variants; verify renderer dependencies, fonts, Arabic shaping, and licensing of any added assets.
