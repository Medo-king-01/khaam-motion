## What it is
SVG icons for popular brands (3475 icons).

## Key capabilities
- Brand/platform/tool logos as clean SVGs
- Useful for explainers, promos, comparisons, tech/tool callouts
- Easy recolor/scale in motion graphics pipelines

## How to use in Khaam
Use when a scene needs a recognizable brand/platform mark (apps, services, dev tools, social platforms). Prefer official/neutral presentation; do not imply endorsement. For Khaam, recolor and animate SVG paths/shapes as needed; keep Arabic labels separate from logo marks.

## Notes
- CC0 makes reuse straightforward for private/internal Khaam work.
- Preserve upstream LICENSE.md and attribution-friendly provenance in delivery manifests when adapted.
- Brand trademarks/logos may still be subject to trademark rights even when icon artwork is CC0; use marks appropriately.
- Apply Khaam rules for Arabic typography, sound discipline, pace, and QA.
